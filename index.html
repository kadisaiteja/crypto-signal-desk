Trade plan (ATR based)

Stop = 1.5×ATR. Targets come from the chart: support/resistance levels, Fibonacci extensions of the recent swing, and the Bollinger band. R = reward divided by risk. Size the position so a stop-out risks 1–2% of your account.

Multi-timeframe

Best setups: higher timeframes agree with the one you trade.

Backtest (loaded candles)


Enters on BUY (and SELL unless long only), exits when the signal fades or the ATR stop hits. Tight stops get whipsawed, so try 2–4. 0.1% fee per side. Past results do not predict future ones.
